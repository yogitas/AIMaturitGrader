Scoring and Interpretation Guide

For each stage, count the number of Yes answers (N/A questions are excluded from the denominator).
The highest stage where 75% or more of applicable questions are answered Yes is the current maturity stage for this process.

Note: A process must satisfy lower-stage thresholds before higher stages are considered. If Stage 0 is fully true, the process is Manual regardless of other answers.
